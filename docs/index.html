Echo

Echo gives you agents that each learn one job from you and keep what they learn. You create an agent, show it the job in a chat and correct it; it writes down the workflows it learns and uses them again next time. Underneath, every agent has a workspace of its own where it runs commands, installs packages, edits files and commits to git, inside an isolated jail, so an agent can only touch its own workspace.

You sign in with your Microsoft work account and bring your own model provider key: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or any service with an OpenAI-compatible API. Your agents and keys are yours alone; nobody else signed in to Echo can see them.

What you can do today

Echo is in early development. Open it in your browser to sign in, add your provider keys, create agents, read and edit their files, see what changed since the last commit, and chat with them. Everything is also available through the API.
